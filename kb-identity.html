NOTE 12 / Identity & access

Risky sign-ins: evidence before verdict_

Study basis: Scenario study

THE PRINCIPLE

Treat a risk signal as a hypothesis, then choose a proportionate response using confidence and active harm.

THE CONNECTION

Impossible travel can reflect a VPN. Conversely, “always confirm before containing” is too rigid when strong evidence indicates an attack is still causing harm.

Explain the signal

An impossible-travel or atypical-location signal compares activity that appears geographically inconsistent. IP geolocation describes the observed network exit, not a GPS reading from the person. VPNs, proxies, mobile networks, and incomplete context can affect interpretation.

Examine the account, timestamps, source addresses, application, device information, authentication details, and surrounding activity. Compare the event with the user's expected work and verify their explanation through a trusted channel where appropriate. A plausible VPN explanation should be checked against the evidence.

Ask each system the right question

  • Entra sign-in records: what identity access occurred and what authentication or policy details were recorded?
  • Intune: what managed-device and compliance information is available?
  • Microsoft 365 audit evidence: what relevant cloud-service actions followed?
  • EDR: what execution or other activity occurred on the endpoint?

Entra identity administration and Intune device management are related but different responsibilities. A cloud account attack does not necessarily produce a malicious local process tree.

Avoid absolute response rules

Immediately resetting every account with a location alert can disrupt legitimate work without resolving the cause. Waiting for perfect certainty during an active compromise can also increase harm. Evaluate confidence, ongoing activity, asset sensitivity, and the reversibility of the proposed control. Follow the runbook and escalation authority.

Where authorized and justified, account restrictions or session revocation may limit further access while investigation continues. Verify the effect: password changes, token lifetimes, and session controls are not interchangeable guarantees.

Leave an evidence-based handoff

Document the alert, corroborating facts, alternative explanations, actions, and remaining uncertainty. “Unusual location; VPN explanation verified against expected activity” conveys more than “false positive” without supporting detail.

CLOSE THE NOTES. MAKE THE CONNECTION.

Recall check

Should every impossible-travel alert immediately trigger a password reset?

Reveal the reasoning

No. Correlate the signal with authentication, device, application, and user context. Escalate or contain promptly when confidence and active harm justify it, within the incident runbook.
