NOTE 07 / Security operations

Blackpoint: MDR is a service_

Study basis: Concept study

THE PRINCIPLE

Distinguish the technology collecting evidence from the people and service responsible for investigating and responding.

THE CONNECTION

EDR, SIEM, and MDR describe different roles. A managed detection service cannot be understood simply as another name for a log dashboard.

Separate three roles

TermCentral roleUseful question
EDREndpoint detection, investigation, and responseWhat executed on the device?
SIEMCollect, search, and correlate security eventsHow do events across systems relate?
MDRA managed detection and response serviceWho investigates and takes action?

These roles can work together. A SIEM is not automatically a staffed response service, and an EDR deployment does not by itself establish continuous human monitoring.

Where Blackpoint fits

Blackpoint offers managed detection and response, including human-led investigation and response services. Treating it simply as “the SIEM” misses the service model. Exactly which systems are monitored and which actions are authorized depends on the purchased service, integrations, and agreed operating arrangements.

A vendor overview demonstrates the concept; it does not establish familiarity with a customer's console, response contract, or escalation process.

Make a handoff useful

A local analyst reviewing an MDR escalation should identify the affected account or asset, the evidence supporting the conclusion, the time window, and actions already performed. Separate confirmed facts from unresolved questions. Record who owns the next step and whether containment has been verified.

For example, an endpoint alert showing suspicious execution may be supported by a firewall event and an unusual account sign-in. Those signals strengthen the timeline when their timestamps and entities align; merely appearing in several products does not prove they belong to the same incident.

Avoid duplicated or conflicting response

Before resetting an account or isolating a device, establish whether the service has already acted and what the runbook authorizes. A complete handoff makes the incident state explicit so the next analyst can continue from evidence.

CLOSE THE NOTES. MAKE THE CONNECTION.

Recall check

Does installing an EDR agent mean an organization now has MDR?

Reveal the reasoning

No. MDR adds a managed operational service. The responsible team, coverage, escalation arrangements, and response authority must be established.
