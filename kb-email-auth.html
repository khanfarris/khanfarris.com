NOTE 10 / Security operations

Email authentication & alignment_

Study basis: Scenario study

THE PRINCIPLE

An authenticated sending domain does not prove that an email's request is legitimate.

THE CONNECTION

A lookalike domain may pass its own authentication. A compromised real mailbox may also send authenticated mail. Both explain why “pass” is not a verdict.

Separate the checks

  • SPF: checks whether the sending system is authorized for the evaluated envelope-sender domain (or HELO identity where applicable).
  • DKIM: verifies a domain's cryptographic signature over the covered message content. It establishes the signing domain and integrity of the signed portions, not the honesty of a human sender.
  • DMARC: evaluates whether a passing SPF or DKIM result aligns with the visible From domain and publishes handling policy for failures.

DMARC does not require both SPF and DKIM to pass. A qualifying aligned pass from either mechanism can satisfy it. A DKIM signature's signing domain can differ from the address the reader sees; alignment is the connection that must be checked.

Compare three suspicious messages

SituationWhy authentication is insufficient
Exact-domain spoofMay fail DMARC when neither passing mechanism aligns; inspect actual results and receiving policy.
Lookalike domainThe attacker can authenticate a domain they control while imitating another organization's name.
Compromised legitimate mailboxThe real account can send authenticated messages containing fraudulent instructions.

Use the right evidence source

Inspect the message headers and trusted receiving system's authentication results through the mail investigation tooling. Compare the full From and Reply-To addresses, links, attachments, and the requested business action. Do not treat arbitrary attacker-supplied header text as a trusted verdict.

Endpoint telemetry may show a downloaded file executing, but it is not the primary record of a cloud mail service's SPF, DKIM, or DMARC evaluation. A SIEM may contain those results if the relevant mail logs are integrated.

Return to the business request

Urgency, payment changes, and requests to bypass normal approval remain significant even when authentication passes. Independently verify a sensitive request through a known contact route and preserve the original message for investigation.

CLOSE THE NOTES. MAKE THE CONNECTION.

Recall check

A CEO payment request passes DMARC. Is it safe to approve?

Reveal the reasoning

No. A lookalike domain or compromised legitimate account can send authenticated mail. Check the actual domain and verify the request through an established independent channel.
