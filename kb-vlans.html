NOTE 04 / Networking

VLANs_

Concept Concept study

IN PLAIN ENGLISH

A VLAN makes one physical switch behave like several separate local networks. Devices share a local broadcast network only when their ports belong to the same VLAN.

Picture an office switch

A switch connects laptops, printers, and other devices by cable. Without separation, those devices may all share one local network. A Virtual Local Area Network assigns ports to separate groups. Staff can be in VLAN 10, guests in VLAN 20, and servers in VLAN 30, even when they connect to the same switch.

A broadcast is a message sent to every device in a local broadcast domain. For example, an ARP request asks which device owns a particular local IP. The switch floods that broadcast within its VLAN, not into every VLAN. This separation is the basic job of a VLAN.

INTERACTIVE / FICTIONAL EXAMPLE

One switch. Separate networks.

Move the guest laptop between VLANs, then test its path to the server. Watch which boundary needs a routing policy.

Ports, tags, and subnets

TermMeaning
Access portNormally carries one VLAN for an end device. The attached laptop usually sends untagged frames.
Trunk portCarries multiple VLANs between network devices, commonly using 802.1Q tags to identify each VLAN.
SubnetThe IP address range used on a network. A common office design assigns a different subnet to each VLAN.
Inter-VLAN routingA router or Layer 3 switch moves traffic between VLANs. Firewall rules or access control lists can restrict that traffic.

A VLAN and a subnet are different settings. The VLAN is the local Ethernet separation; the subnet tells IP devices what is local. They are normally planned together. Giving a laptop an address from a different subnet does not move its switch port into another VLAN.

What actually protects the server?

If guests and servers are in different VLANs, a guest cannot directly reach the server through ordinary same-VLAN switching. But if a router connects the two networks and allows the traffic, the guest can still reach the server. The intended policy might allow guests to use the internet while denying connections to server addresses.

If an administrator accidentally places a guest port in the server VLAN, traffic may stay inside that VLAN and bypass the inter-VLAN firewall. Check VLAN membership as well as the rules between networks. Hosts and applications also need their own controls.

Use it on a client ticket

  1. Confirm the device’s IP, mask, gateway, and physical switch port.
  2. Check the port’s VLAN and whether the expected VLAN is allowed on upstream trunks.
  3. Check that this VLAN has the right gateway and DHCP scope or relay.
  4. For cross-VLAN access, inspect the route and the rule for the specific source, destination, protocol, and port.
  5. After an approved change, verify the intended connection works and a forbidden connection remains blocked.
Public IPs are separate

Staff and guest VLANs can share the same public IP for internet access. A VLAN creates local separation, not a new internet subscription.

PUT IT INTO WORDS

Check your understanding

A guest VLAN exists, but guests can still open the file server. What would you investigate?

Show an example answer

Check that the guest is actually on the guest VLAN, then inspect routing and firewall or ACL rules between guests and servers. Separate VLANs alone do not guarantee a deny policy. Verify the exact service and both allowed and blocked paths.
