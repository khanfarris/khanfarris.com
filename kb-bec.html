NOTE 11 / Security operations

BEC: verify before payment_

Study basis: Scenario study

THE PRINCIPLE

Break the attacker's communication path when validating a sensitive business request.

THE CONNECTION

Replying to the suspicious CEO email asks the same potentially compromised channel to vouch for itself. A known phone number provides a separate verification path.

Identify the attempted outcome

Business Email Compromise is a fraud scenario, not a defensive framework. An attacker impersonates a trusted party or uses a compromised account to induce a payment, disclosure, or other business action. Malware is not required.

An urgent executive request to transfer funds should trigger the established approval process. Pause the transaction while verifying through a known contact number or another approved independent channel. A number supplied inside the suspicious message is not independent verification.

Preserve and assess the message

Retain the original message and headers. Examine the full sender and reply addresses, authentication results, links, attachments, and message history. Authentication supports domain assessment but does not prove the request is authorized. A legitimate account may have been compromised.

Scope beyond one recipient

Determine who else received the message, whether anyone replied or submitted information, and whether a payment was initiated. If account compromise is suspected, examine sign-ins, relevant mailbox audit activity, forwarding settings, and inbox rules using available permissions and tooling.

Endpoint investigation is appropriate where a recipient opened a file or executed content. A cloud-only mailbox compromise may leave its strongest evidence in identity and mail-service logs instead.

Respond and communicate

Apply the incident runbook and approved authority for account containment, session revocation, credential recovery, or mail remediation. Coordinate promptly with the responsible financial team and institution if money has moved. Record the time, evidence, impact, actions, and outstanding questions.

A useful update explains the observed request, the verified risk, and the next action in plain language. It does not substitute an unconfirmed conclusion for evidence or blame the person who reported the message.

CLOSE THE NOTES. MAKE THE CONNECTION.

Recall check

What is the first verification step for an unexpected urgent transfer request?

Reveal the reasoning

Pause the transaction and contact the supposed requester through an established independent channel. Preserve the message and investigate the technical evidence alongside the business verification.
